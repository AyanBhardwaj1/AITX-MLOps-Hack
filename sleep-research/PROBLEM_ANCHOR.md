# Problem anchor (frozen)

A founder at any startup bank should be able to get a **correct** runway and a **trustworthy** read of their wasted spend in seconds, without connecting an account. Every finding should hold up on the messy ledger shapes real companies have, not just on the generator it was tuned on. The core hypothesis has two parts:
1. Runway's detectors break in specific, fixable ways on realistic ledger shapes. The stress test found three: usage-priced growth, recurring same-day workspace pairs, and refunded double charges. Fixing them restores ≥ 90% precision without losing recall.
2. The same engine, fed a bank CSV export in the browser, reproduces the connected-account runway and findings closely enough to serve as the free front door.

Later stages may sharpen how this is done, but not swap the problem: trust on unfamiliar ledgers, plus reach beyond Rho through a no-signup grader.
